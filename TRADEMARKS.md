The code in this repository is shared under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`). You're welcome to copy it, change it and use it for your own site.

These are **not** covered by that license and stay with their owner:

- The names **Smart Cubby** and **Pigeon**, and the smart-cubby.com domain.
- The **pigeon mascot** artwork (the cartoon pigeon with the monocle and graduation cap) and the Smart Cubby look as a whole.
- Everything under **`public/schools/`**: the Smart Cubby product pages, the faculty demo, the overview PDF and the cabinet photos.
