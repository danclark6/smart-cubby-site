How It Works

1. Real-Time Status

An LED light on each slot shows class status live: Red means the student is in class, and Green means they are in a free period.

2. At-a-Glance Compliance

Teachers and administrators can instantly check compliance from down the hall. If a slot is red and empty, the phone is missing.

3. Grade-Level Flexibility

Set custom policies by grade. Keep phones docked all day for underclassmen, or allow free-period access for upperclassmen.

Simple Administration & Privacy First

Privacy by Coordinates

Slots use coordinate numbers (like A14 or A15) instead of student names, ensuring complete identity privacy for anyone walking down the hall.

Blackbaud SKY API Integration

Syncs directly with your school management system to track student class schedules and free periods automatically in real time.

Simple Accountability

Staff scan the cabinet's QR code and tap an empty slot to log an issue. Automated end-of-day emails keep students, parents, and deans informed without classroom friction.

Why a Cabinet Instead of Phone Pouches?

Feature Pigeon Smart Cubby Traditional Pouches (e.g. Yondr)
Teacher Workload Zero classroom disruption. Monitoring happens quickly in hallway corridors. High friction. Teachers must spot violations, stop teaching, and handle administrative paperwork.
Tamper Resistance Easy to check, hard to fake. Visual lights ensure immediate compliance visibility. Bypassed easily using cheap magnets or physical force.
Long-Term Value Durable physical unit with flexible wall-mount or mobile rolling configurations. Ongoing replacement costs due to lost, damaged, or stolen pouches over time.
Habit Building Teaches students the real-world skill of putting phones away in a designated spot to focus. Relies on artificial locking devices that students will never encounter outside of school.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the unit permanent or portable?

It can be built either way. Smart Cubby cabinets can be mounted stationary on hallway walls or configured on heavy-duty wheels to roll into a storage closet when needed. It is modular too so cabinets come in a variety of cubby counts.

How much time does monitoring take for staff?

Very little. Assigned staff take less than a minute to check cubbies two or three times a day. Because compliance is visible at a glance, any staff member walking past can also check status easily.

How does the system know when a student has a free period?

The system connects with your school management system (via Blackbaud SKY API) to automatically update individual cubby LED lights based on the student's live schedule.
